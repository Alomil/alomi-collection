ALOMI COLLECTION

Step in Style. Step with Alomi.

ALOMI COLLECTION is an online footwear and fashion accessories store based in Jobele, Oyo State, Nigeria.

Products

- Men's Shoes
- Women's Shoes
- Unisex Shoes
- Couple Shoes
- Leather Shoes and Slippers
- Sandals and Slides
- Bags

Planned Features

- Customer accounts and an owner admin dashboard
- Product catalogue and product ratings
- Delivery information and local government selection
- Payment and bank transfer options
- Customer notifications
- Mobile-friendly shopping experience

Important: This repository is being prepared for the app source code. The features listed above are planned and are not yet implemented by this README.
